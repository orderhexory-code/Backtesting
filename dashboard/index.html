1. Multi-Timeframe Strategy Hierarchy
The strategy operates on a strict multi-timeframe confirmation pipeline to filter market noise and isolate high-probability trend expansions:
Execution Sequence
1H Completed Reference → 15M Directional Intent → 5M Breakdown/Breakout Confirmation → 1M Entry Execution → Dynamic Multi-R Trailing Stop
2. Step 1: 1-Hour Completed Reference Framework

At the close of every hourly bar, the engine marks two critical institutional liquidity boundary levels:

  • Reference High: The exact high price reached during the completed 1-Hour candle.
  • Reference Low: The exact low price reached during the completed 1-Hour candle.

Anti-Lookahead Rule: An incomplete 1H candle is never used. The boundaries become valid only after the exact minute :00 hour close.

3. Step 2: 15-Minute Directional Classification
After the 1-Hour reference is finalized, subsequent completed 15-Minute bars determine market intent across 4 distinct categories:
A. 15M Upside Breakout (LONG Intent)
Condition: 15M High > 1H High AND 15M Close > 1H High. Confirms buyers have accepted prices above the reference level. Sets a directional plan for LONG.
B. 15M Downside Breakout (SHORT Intent)
Condition: 15M Low < 1H Low AND 15M Close < 1H Low. Confirms sellers have accepted prices below the reference level. Sets a directional plan for SHORT.
C. 15M Upside Sweep / Liquidity Grab (Potential SHORT Intent)
Condition: 15M High > 1H High AND 15M Close <= 1H High. Indicates buyer rejection (liquidity sweep). Prepares for a short entry pending downside confirmation.
D. 15M Downside Sweep / Liquidity Grab (Potential LONG Intent)
Condition: 15M Low < 1H Low AND 15M Close >= 1H Low. Indicates seller rejection (liquidity sweep). Prepares for a long entry pending upside confirmation.
4. Step 3: 5-Minute Confirmation Protocol

Once a 15-minute directional plan is established, the engine awaits a completed 5-minute candle to confirm directional continuation past the reference extreme level.

Hard Rule on 5-Minute Sweeps
5-Minute wicks or sweeps are strictly ignored as independent reversal signals. Only the 15-Minute timeframe has the authority to dictate directional sweeps or trend changes. The 5-Minute timeframe is used purely for structural confirmation.
5. Step 4: 1-Minute Entry Execution & Stop Loss
  • Execution Mode: Next Bar Open (`next_bar_open`). When 5M confirmation occurs, entry triggers immediately on the opening tick of the subsequent 1-minute candle.
  • Structural Initial Stop Loss: Placed at the lowest swing point (for Longs) or highest swing point (for Shorts) of the preceding 1-minute structure + 1 buffer tick.
  • Slippage & Cost Model: Every simulated execution includes realistic slippage and broker commissions deducted directly from gross P&L.
6. Step 5: Dynamic Multi-R Trailing Stop Protocol

To eliminate arbitrary profit targets and capture extended runner trends, the strategy enforces strict non-partial multi-R trailing:

Stage 1 (+1R Milestone)
When price reaches +1R in profit: The Stop Loss is instantly moved to +1R in Profit. The position remains 100% open with zero partial profit taken.
Stage 2 (+3R Milestone)
When price reaches +3R in profit: The Stop Loss advances to +3R in Profit, securing a significant win while letting the trade run.
Stage 3 (Post +3R Trailing Ladder)
For every subsequent full +1R advancement (4R, 5R, 6R, 7R...): The Stop Loss trails forward by +1R (+4R, +5R, +6R...) until the market reverses into the trailing stop. There is no fixed take profit.
7. Step 6: Risk Management & Multi-Trade Lifecycle
  • Dynamic Compounding Position Sizing: Contract sizing is calculated as a fixed percentage of current total account equity, automatically scaling position sizes as profits grow.
  • Session & Hourly Boundary Survival: An active trade is never closed merely because a new hour opens or the trading session rolls over. Each trade manages its lifecycle independently until its trailing stop is hit.
  • Concurrent Trade Independence: If a new valid hourly reference setup triggers while an older trade is still trailing, both trades run simultaneously with independent stop loss and risk tracking.
