1. Multi-Timeframe Strategy Hierarchy
The strategy operates on a chronological, event-driven confirmation pipeline to isolate genuine institutional trend expansions while avoiding false breakout noise:
Execution Sequence
1-Hour Completed Reference → 15-Minute Directional Intent → 5-Minute Breakdown/Breakout Confirmation → 1-Minute Entry Execution → Dynamic Multi-R Trailing Stop
2. Step 1: 1-Hour Completed Reference Framework

At the close of every hourly bar, the engine records two benchmark levels:

  • Reference High: The exact high reached by the completed 1-Hour candle.
  • Reference Low: The exact low reached by the completed 1-Hour candle.

Anti-Lookahead Rule: An incomplete 1H candle is never used. Levels become active strictly after the completed :00 minute mark.

3. Step 2: 15-Minute Directional Classification
Subsequent completed 15-Minute bars evaluate price interaction against the 1-Hour reference:
A. 15M Upside Breakout (LONG Intent)
Condition: 15M High > 1H High AND 15M Close > 1H High. Buyers accept price above reference level. Sets a LONG plan.
B. 15M Downside Breakout (SHORT Intent)
Condition: 15M Low < 1H Low AND 15M Close < 1H Low. Sellers accept price below reference level. Sets a SHORT plan.
C. 15M Upside Sweep / Liquidity Grab (Potential SHORT Intent)
Condition: 15M High > 1H High AND 15M Close <= 1H High. Indicates rejection above high. Prepares for Short entry upon downside confirmation.
D. 15M Downside Sweep / Liquidity Grab (Potential LONG Intent)
Condition: 15M Low < 1H Low AND 15M Close >= 1H Low. Indicates rejection below low. Prepares for Long entry upon upside confirmation.
4. Step 3: 5-Minute Confirmation Protocol

Following a 15-minute directional plan, the engine requires a completed 5-minute bar to confirm momentum past the reference extreme level.

Hard Rule: 5-Minute Sweeps Ignored
5-Minute wicks or sweeps are strictly ignored as independent signals. Only the 15-Minute timeframe determines directional intent. 5-Minute is used exclusively for confirmation.
5. Step 4: 1-Minute Entry Execution & Stop Loss
  • Execution Mode: Next Bar Open (`next_bar_open`). When 5M confirmation occurs, entry executes on the opening tick of the subsequent 1-minute candle.
  • Structural Initial Stop Loss: Placed at the lowest swing point (for Longs) or highest swing point (for Shorts) of the preceding 1-minute structure + 1 buffer tick.
  • Execution Costs: Slippage and exchange commissions are automatically deducted from net P&L.
6. Step 5: Dynamic Multi-R Trailing Stop Protocol

To capture extended trend legs without premature exits, the strategy applies non-partial dynamic trailing stops:

Stage 1 (+1R Milestone)
When price reaches +1R in profit: Stop Loss is immediately moved to +1R in Profit. The position remains 100% open with zero partial exits.
Stage 2 (+3R Milestone)
When price reaches +3R in profit: Stop Loss advances to +3R in Profit, locking in gains while letting runners expand.
Stage 3 (Post +3R Trailing Ladder)
For every subsequent full +1R advancement (4R, 5R, 6R...): Stop Loss trails forward by +1R (+4R, +5R, +6R...) until stopped out. There is no fixed take profit.
7. Step 6: Risk Management & Multi-Trade Lifecycle
  • Dynamic Compounding Position Sizing: Contract size is dynamically calculated as a percentage of current account equity, scaling lot sizes automatically as profits grow.
  • Session & Hourly Boundary Persistence: Active trades are never prematurely closed due to hourly or session boundaries. Each trade manages its lifecycle independently until stopped out.
